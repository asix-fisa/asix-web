ASIX: Analog Spectral Imager for X-rays¶

ASIX (Analog Spectral Imager for X-Rays) will deliver a new class of hybrid, room-temperature X-ray detectors for single-photon, event-driven, spectral imaging, suitable for large data throughput applications for space observations and material science.
